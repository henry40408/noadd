Step 9: upstream forwarder (src/upstream/forwarder.rs, tests/upstream_test.rs)

Decisions pinned by the Rust wire fixtures (rustWireCases in internal/dns/wire_cases_test.go, 062-065, 076-086, 092):
- UDP TC=1 is a failure, not a TCP retry. The Rust pool (hickory) had no TCP
  connection to retry on, so a truncated upstream answer ended as SERVFAIL for
  the client. The plan said "TC -> TCP retry"; that was overridden by the
  fixtures. No 0x20 case randomisation either.
- Failure rcodes follow hickory: SERVFAIL, REFUSED, FORMERR, NOTIMP, BADVERS and
  truncation fail over to the next upstream. NXDOMAIN / NODATA are negative
  answers and are rebuilt (authority section kept, no AA/AD, OPT only if the
  client sent one).
- Responses to a client without DO lose DNSSEC records and AD; OPT is removed in
  prepare and re-added only when the client sent one.
- Hostnames resolve once at startup to the first address (geo-routed PoP pin).

Deviations:
- tests/upstream_test.rs hit real resolvers; the Go tests use local UDP, DoT
  and DoH fakes (self-signed, injected TLSConfig and Resolver).
- Time-dependent constants come through Config (plan rule 3).
